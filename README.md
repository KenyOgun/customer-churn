# Customer Churn Analytics Dashboard – Databel Telecom Insights

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Customer Churn Analytics solution developed in Power BI for a fictional telecommunications company, Databel. The objective of the project was to analyze customer churn behavior, identify key drivers of churn, and design interactive dashboards that support data-driven retention strategies.

The project followed a structured analytics workflow including:

- Data quality validation and exploratory data analysis (EDA)
- Data transformation and calculated field creation
- KPI and churn metric development using DAX
- Interactive dashboard and report design
- Customer segmentation and churn pattern analysis
- Insight storytelling for business stakeholders

The dataset consisted of customer demographic information, contract details, payment methods, service usage patterns, and churn indicators. The dashboard consolidated these metrics into a centralized reporting solution to help stakeholders better understand customer retention risks and opportunities.

---

## Business Goals

The primary business objective was to help Databel understand:

- Why customers churn and the primary drivers behind attrition
- Which customer segments are at the highest risk of churn
- How contract types, pricing, customer service interactions, and data usage influence churn behavior
- Geographic churn trends across different states
- The relationship between customer demographics and churn probability
- Opportunities to improve customer retention and loyalty

The project aimed to provide actionable insights that could improve:

- Customer retention strategies
- Contract and pricing decisions
- Marketing and promotional targeting
- Customer service effectiveness
- Product and plan optimization
- Strategic business decision-making

## Key Insights Enabled

The dashboard enabled several strategic customer retention insights, including:

- Databel’s overall churn rate was approximately 27%
- Customers on month-to-month contracts had significantly higher churn rates (~46%)
- Two-year contract customers showed very low churn rates (~2.78%)
- California exhibited the highest churn rate at approximately 64%
- Competitor-related issues were identified as the leading churn category
- Senior citizens churned at rates significantly above the average
- Customers without long-term contracts and those with high service usage showed elevated churn risk
- Customers with unlimited plans and low data usage displayed unexpectedly high churn behavior
- Group plans were associated with lower monthly charges and lower churn rates
